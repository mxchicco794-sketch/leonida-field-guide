# 3D asset licence manifest

Every external 3D asset (model, texture, HDR, sound) used by the 3D map must be listed here with its source URL, author, licence and date verified. Only CC0 or other compatible licences; no AGPL; no GTA VI game assets.

| Asset | Source | Author | Licence | Verified |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _(none)_ | — | — | — | — |

All 3D geometry currently comes from the Open GTA 6 Map data (https://github.com/fbritoferreira/gta6, CC BY 4.0): coastline, surface areas, building footprints, roads, rails and districts. Building heights, roof setbacks and colours are generated by documented, stylized rules in `src/lib/leonida/geo3d/buildings.ts`. They are not official data.
